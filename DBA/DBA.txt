Since my company is small and operates using funding provided by the university, it is best to use cloud services such as Amazon Web Services (AWS). This eliminates the need for a dedicated employee to manage backups, performance, and other aspects of the database. I am not offering full DBA services, as many of these responsibilities will be managed by AWS and partially by myself. AWS was chosen due to its ability to scale as the company grows, as well as its managed database services, which reduce overhead. Security is managed in part by AWS. They provide data encryption, monitor aspects of the database, and restrict access so that only approved users can interact with the system through Identity and Access Management (IAM) roles. Backup is also handled by AWS. They provide automatic daily backups and store those backups on their own servers, ensuring that the database is protected in case of failure. The system can also be restored to nearly any point in time, as long as a backup exists, through point-in-time recovery.
